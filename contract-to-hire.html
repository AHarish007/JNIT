Contract to Hire Staffing

Evaluate talent on the job before making a full-time commitment, for better hiring decisions with less risk.

Talk to Our ExpertsAll Services

Overview

Hire with confidence

Contract to hire lets a professional start with your team on contract. You see their skills, work style and fit in real work before deciding on a permanent offer.

For candidates, it is a chance to experience your team and culture first. For you, it turns a hiring decision into an informed one.

  • Evaluate skills in real work before committing
  • Reduce the risk of a poor hire
  • Convert strong performers to full-time
