VitalSigns

Monitoring, Administration & Reporting Solutions for Collaborative Technologies and Hybrid Environments

VitalSigns is a next generation Monitoring tool that serves both IT as the Business.

The Solution is gaining visibility into each service endpoint, therefore helping to proactively manage environmental health and availability, round the clock. Businesses depend entirely on systems and their availability. VitalSigns helps IT departments to successfully track and discover issue before end users do. Improved performance, less downtime, less support tickets and increased reputation of IT.

Hoozin

A Digital Transformation Solution Helping Organizations Move from an Intranet Culture to a Digital Workplace Culture

Office 365

Employee engagement and user adoption starts here

Transform communication with a high impact collaborative Intranet

Digital Workplace

Digital Workplace with application integration and Single Sign On

Hoozin is a solution that allows building next generation Digital experiences using UI and Devops in a unique manner.

Hoozin offers a two-stage approach to digital collaboration. In the first stage, Hoozin provides an out-of-the-box Intranet with a social stream and the ability to create custom Communities with its landing pages. Also in this first stage, organizations can easily customize their Intranet by adding out-of-the-box widgets with a simple click.

The second stage builds upon the first by integrating all third-party productivity applications, such as Office 365, Salesforce, SAP, etc. into a consolidated, single sign-on Digital Workplace.
