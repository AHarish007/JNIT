Service overview

Permanent hires, placed with care

Direct staffing is how JNIT helps you build your core team. We find, screen and present full-time candidates who match both the requirements of the role and the way your team works.

You make the final decision and the candidate joins your payroll from day one. We handle the search so your managers can stay focused on the work.

  • Full-time placements for technical and business roles
  • Candidates screened for skills and culture fit
  • Access to top global talent
