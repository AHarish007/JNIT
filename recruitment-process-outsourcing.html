What is RPO?

Your recruitment function, managed end to end

Recruitment process outsourcing (RPO) means JNIT takes responsibility for all or part of your recruitment process. We act as an extension of your team, managing sourcing, screening, coordination and onboarding.

RPO brings structure and consistency to hiring, which matters most when volume grows or when your internal team is stretched.

  • End-to-end or partial recruitment management
  • Consistent process and candidate experience
  • Scales with your hiring volume
